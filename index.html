Who this applies to

This guide applies to borrowers who already have a property or loan goal and need to organise the next conversation around lender policy, serviceability, documents and timing. It is not a substitute for personal credit advice.

  • First home buyers: ask how deposit size, income, expenses, NSW or federal scheme settings and exact property details should be checked before relying on an application path.
  • Refinancers: prepare the current lender, loan balance, repayment type, equity position and likely switching costs.
  • Investors: ask how rental income, tax settings and lender policy may affect the proposed structure.
  • Construction borrowers: identify whether the plan is a knockdown-rebuild, house-and-land purchase or major build, then ask how progress payments will be assessed.
  • Self-employed borrowers: prepare income records that explain sole trader, contractor, business or company income.
  • Bridging borrowers: set out whether the issue is buying before selling, lining up settlement dates or managing a short-term finance gap.
